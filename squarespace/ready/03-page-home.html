The STIR approach

The STIR approach

01Read the room.

Every business has its own rhythm. We get close to the people, the product, and the customers to understand what’s already working—and what deserves more attention.

A clear direction.

02Set the scene.

We give that direction a form people can recognize. The identity, the content, the website: each piece helps tell the story and gives people a reason to take the next step.

Work ready to meet the world.

03Make it travel.

We put the work where people can find it. For ongoing partners, we keep creating, learn from the response, and improve the path from first impression to inquiry or order.

A presence that keeps moving.
