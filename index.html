What we do01

A business with something worthwhile, and a partner with room to shape it.

Most of this work is connected: the way a business is positioned shows up in its content, and the content only pays if the website and the channels behind it can take the weight. STIR works across those at once rather than handing over one piece and leaving.

A clearly defined single project is a valid way to start. Scope, responsibilities and capacity are agreed for each engagement — LABAY shows how wide the relationship can go, not what every engagement includes.

STIR is building a network of creators and collaborators around the work. A very small following is welcome; a relevant perspective or a specific contribution can matter more than audience size. Expressing interest is a review for future project fit — it is not an open role, a placement or a promise of payment.
