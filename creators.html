Bring your perspective.

If you make or publish content in your own voice — including UGC, and including a very small or emerging audience — there is a route here.

There is no follower minimum, no engagement-rate floor, and no requirement to have a public audience at all. Strong work, a relevant perspective, or a specific idea can matter more than reach.

You can describe something you want to make rather than send a portfolio. A private sample is fine. There is no unpaid test assignment.

What this is. Expressing interest means your details are considered for relevant future projects, and STIR reaches out when there is a fit. It is not an application to an open role, a promise of selection, or an agreed rate. Compensation and terms are discussed for actual projects.
