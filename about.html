STIR is a founder-led creative practice. I make content, campaigns, brands and websites for businesses with a street address — the kind people walk past, call, or order from. I have run one myself.

Why the two halves line up

Most creative work arrives from outside the business and leaves again. The reason the judgment and the operating reality line up here is that both have been done in the same place: the brand and the business it belongs to.

LABAY Market is the flagship case for exactly that reason — it is where creative direction, content, the website and the wholesale side are all one job.

The network around the work

STIR is building a group of creators and collaborators around the projects rather than an agency staff. A very small following is welcome; a relevant perspective or one specific contribution can matter more than audience size.

Expressing interest is a review for future project fit. It is not an open role, a placement, or an agreed rate.
