06 / How we work

We agree on the priorities, make the work, and keep improving what needs ongoing attention.

We look at the product, the people, and how the business works. Then we agree on what needs to be communicated and what the work should help people do.

We create the content, identity, or website around that brief, with a clear scope and points for your input.

For ongoing work, we manage the agreed channels and updates, review the response, and use inquiries and sales data where available to guide the next round.

07 / Who this is for

We work with businesses that already do something well.

You have something worth choosing. You need more people to understand it, a clearer way to buy, or help keeping the work moving.

Our strongest experience is in food, hospitality, and independent businesses. The same attention to clear communication carries into our website work across other fields.
