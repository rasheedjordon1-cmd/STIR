Product arrives inside the DC's window. Documented. No chargeback.

Any warehouse can keep a box cold. The money is lost at the receiving door: a missed appointment, a short ASN, a lot code that doesn't match the paperwork. This operation is built around that door.

Delivery appointments
Every outbound load is scheduled against the DC's receiving window before it leaves the dock. The appointment number rides on the bill of lading.On the load: the appointment number and window travel on the bill of lading.
OTIF
Loads are built and dispatched to land on time and in full. A late load and a short load cost you the same chargeback, so both are treated as failures here, not at your retailer's deduction desk.Scored by: your retailer's vendor scorecard, delivery by delivery.
ASN and EDI
Advance ship notices and EDI transmission: TK. Whether notices originate from this warehouse or from your team is being confirmed and will be stated here plainly.On the wire: an 856 advance ship notice tells the DC what is on the truck before it arrives.
Lot codes and rotation
Every pallet is received against a lot code and rotated FIFO or FEFO, whichever your retailer's routing guide calls for.On the record: lot code and receipt date against every pallet position.
Temperature record
Zone temperatures are logged by TK. The record is available for any load on request.On request: the zone log for the days your product was in the building.
Recall traceability
Lot-level trace from receipt to the DC door. If a lot is pulled, you know which pallets, which loads, and which docks.On request: pallets, loads, and docks for any lot, from receipt to the DC door.
Lumper coordination
Unloading and lumper fees at the DC are coordinated ahead of the appointment, so the driver isn't negotiating at the door.On the receipt: the lumper fee is agreed before the appointment, not at the door.
Routing guide compliance
Retail routing guide handling: TK.Checked against: pallet height, label placement, and the appointment rules in the guide.
