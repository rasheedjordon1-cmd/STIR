About

Ordinary businesses.
Extraordinary demand.

STIR is an independent growth and creative practice in Brooklyn, New York. We work with businesses most agencies overlook — markets, restaurants, operators, creators — because the interesting part is usually already there. Our job is to find it, make it impossible to ignore, and build the system that turns the attention into revenue.

Independent Brooklyn, New York Est. 2025 @builtbystir

What we believe

People can’t buy what they don’t see.

And being seen is only useful if something happens next. So we don’t sell content. We make the work, put it in front of the right people and build the system that turns their attention into business — more calls, more orders, more bookings, more customers.

The work so far

It’s already working.

Our work with Labay Market earned 10M+ organic views — and the attention turned into bookings and accounts: a Nike Tennis block party, a Hoopbus × Red Bull event on the block, and standing wholesale produce orders from Kabawa and Café Charmant. We’ve worked with creators including Kemoy Martin, Nicolas Nuvan and MarcosWorldNyc, and more join the system every season.

Founder, STIR · Brooklyn, New York

I’ve opened a business, run one, and closed one. That’s the lens I bring to every owner I work with.

I left college to help my father open a restaurant, handling operations, marketing, sales, and design, often in the same day. Later I built a restaurant concept of my own. Creative decisions there had real consequences, and I learned to judge work by what it actually does.

Before that, I started in music at Roc Nation, Universal Music Group / Republic Records, and iHeartRadio, across marketing and A&R. That’s where I learned that a rollout is a system: identity, timing, relationships, and a reason for people to tell someone else.

STIR combines both. I work with operator-owned businesses to find what deserves attention, give it an identity worth remembering, and build the systems that get it discovered, talked about, and bought, from Caribbean food to cold-chain logistics.

At Labay Market, I lead creative direction, content, wholesale accounts, e-commerce, and partnerships for a historic Caribbean neighborhood institution. Two restaurant groups now buy their produce from it — Momofuku’s Kabawa and Catch Hospitality’s Café Charmant. Both came inbound.
