LABAY Market — Brooklyn, New York

Rooted in Grenada.
Reaching beyond the block.

LABAY is a Grenadian market in Brooklyn’s Little Caribbean, with produce, personality, and a story worth spending time with.

STIR leads its creative direction and digital channels, produces short-form content, manages its website and e-commerce work, and coordinates wholesale account relationships.

From the shop floor to the screen.

The work starts inside the business: the fruit arriving, the questions customers ask, and the conversations that happen around the counter.

We turn that material into content and connect it to the places where people can learn more, shop, and stay in touch.
