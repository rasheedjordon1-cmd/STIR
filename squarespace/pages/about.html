About

Good businesses deserve
to be understood.

STIR is an independent creative and growth practice founded by Rasheed Jordon in Brooklyn. We make content, campaigns, identities, and websites, with ongoing support for the work that needs to keep moving.

Independent Brooklyn, New York Est. 2025 @builtbystir

What we believe

Make it travel.

A good product, a useful service, or a place people care about gives us something to work with. We help communicate what matters and make the next step clear for someone who wants to visit, inquire, order, or book.

The work so far.

At LABAY Market, a Grenadian grocer in Brooklyn, our work spans creative direction, content, websites, e-commerce, partnerships, and wholesale coordination.

Our website work also includes Carriacou Logistics, 24 Hour Pipe Patrol, and Temple of Refuge. Different organizations, each with something specific their visitors need to understand or do.

Who you’re talking to

Rasheed Jordon

Founder, STIR · Brooklyn, New York

I’ve opened a business, run one, and closed one. That’s the lens I bring to every owner I work with.

I left college to help my father open a restaurant, handling operations, marketing, sales, and design, often in the same day. Later I built a restaurant concept of my own. Creative decisions there had real consequences, and I learned to judge work by what it actually does.

Before that, I interned in music — at Universal Music Group, Roc Nation, and iHeartRadio. That’s where I learned that a rollout is a system: identity, timing, relationships, and a reason for people to tell someone else.

STIR combines both. I work with operator-owned businesses to find what deserves attention, give it an identity worth remembering, and build the systems that get it discovered, talked about, and bought, from Caribbean food to cold-chain logistics.

At LABAY Market, a Grenadian grocer in Brooklyn, I lead creative direction and digital channels, produce short-form content, manage the website and e-commerce work, and handle wholesale account relationships. That includes being the point of contact for LABAY’s wholesale customers, Kabawa and Café Charmant.
